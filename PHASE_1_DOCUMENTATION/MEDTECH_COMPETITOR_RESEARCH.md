# Competitor Research

## Research method

This is product research, not interface copying. Claims below are from vendor/community primary sources accessed on 2026-11-02.

## Verified observations

| Product | Verified capabilities | MedTech opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practo Ray | Its terms describe booking, reminders, clinical notes, prescriptions, bills, and invoices. Its product materials describe appointment, billing, EMR, and dashboard patterns. [Practo Ray terms](https://www.practo.com/assets/pdf/Practo-Subscriptions-T-and-C.pdf) | Make appointment status and next action visible; keep clinical, billing, and reminders connected without adopting Practo branding. |

[OpenEMR features](https://www.open-emr.org/wiki/index.php/OpenEMR_Features) | Use its breadth as a gap checklist, not a scope promise; prefer staged workflows and robust role/ownership controls. |

## Patterns worth adapting

- Calendar availability plus a clear appointment state machine.
- One patient summary with drill-down rather than scattered pages.
- Clinical note → prescription/lab → billing traceability.
- Task/exception dashboards, not vanity metrics.
- Explicit reminder, consent, and communication preferences.
- Printable, human-readable documents with a digital audit trail.

## Distinct MedTech identity

MedTech should be a calm blue-and-white “care operations workspace”: high information density, plain language, strong status hierarchy, no copied layouts, terminology, assets, or branding.

## Recommendation

Prioritize patient timeline, clinical workspace, and operational exceptions before advanced CRM, inventory, claims clearinghouse, or AI features.
